# Scheduling Skill

Best-practice posting windows (platform-local time) and cadence rules.

## Daily cadence targets (autonomous mode)
- X: 3 posts/day minimum — one morning thought, one mid-afternoon insight, one evening question.
- Instagram: 1 post/day, best 12:00-19:00, never more than 2/day.
- LinkedIn: 1 post/day on weekdays, 9am or 12pm. Skip weekends *unless* cross-posting a banger.
- YouTube: 1-2 long-form/week + community posts 2-3x/week.
- TikTok: 1x/day (evening, 18:00-22:00 works best).
- Telegram: 1 broadcast/day 18:00-20:00.
- WhatsApp: 1 broadcast/week, keep it short.
- Discord: scheduled community prompts 3x/week.
- Reddit: 2-3 posts/week, never at 3am, respect each subreddit's vibe.
- Patreon: 2-3 exclusive posts/week.
- Snapchat: daily streak stories feel best at night.

## Rules
- Never publish two platforms at the exact same minute (spread actions).
- If a platform had zero engagement for 7+ days, reduce cadence and change format, not volume.
- Always keep 1 slot/day free for "reactive" posting to a trending niche topic.
- Schedule counts can be raised only after engagement >= 5% baseline.
