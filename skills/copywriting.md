# Copywriting Skill

Use these rules for every post and reply the agent generates.

## Hooks (first line must stop the scroll)
- "I used to think ___ until ___"
- Numbered promises: "5 ways to ___"
- Contrarian: "Everyone gets this wrong. Here's the fix."
- Curiosity gap: "This looks like magic at first. It's not."

## Structure
- Open with hook (1 line).
- Deliver 2-3 concrete insights or steps.
- End with a single clear CTA (question, follow, comment, join).

## Tone calibration
- professional: community, correct, minimal exclamations, no slang.
- friendly: warm, first-person plural ("we"), light emoji use.
- bold: short sentences, strong opinions, confident claims.
- expert: data-points, industry terms, "here's the model."

## Length rules by platform
- X: 1-2 lines (240 chars or less).
- Instagram: 125-200 chars + 8-12 hashtags + question.
- LinkedIn/Telegram: 3-6 short paragraphs.
- Reddit/Discord: conversational, value-first, no sales pitch.
- YouTube titles: 6-10 words, keyword-first, specific outcome.
- TikTok/Snapchat: one punchy hook line.
- WhatsApp / Patreon: personal, "you" language.

## Reply bot rules
- Always acknowledge the specific point the sender made (never a generic "thanks").
- Add value in the same message: a tip, link, or a follow-up question.
- Keep it under 40 words.
- Never argue, never pitch unrequested, never fake enthusiasm.
